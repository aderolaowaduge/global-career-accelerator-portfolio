Projects

Destination Discovery Prototype

As my final project for the Web Development Track of the Global Career Accelerator program, I developed a prototype for a vacation destination recommendation feature, designed to help users explore new locations based on their interests. The project was inspired by the Marriott Homes and Villas case study provided by Publicis Sapient and challenged me to apply frontend development skills, conduct user research, and think through product management concepts.

Through this project, I focused on creating a more exploration-friendly user experience, helping users discover destinations with clearer guidance and personalized recommendations.
